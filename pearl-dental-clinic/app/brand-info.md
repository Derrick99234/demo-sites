### 7. Clinical Pre-Treatment & Aftercare Guidelines
* **Laser Whitening Preparation:** Eat a normal meal and brush your teeth before coming in (you will not eat for 1 hour post-treatment).
* **The 48-Hour "White Diet" Rule:** For 48 hours following laser whitening, tooth enamel pores are slightly open. Patients must avoid dark/staining food and drinks (no black coffee, red wine, dark tea, curry, tomato stew, soy sauce, or tobacco). Water, milk, white rice, chicken, fish, and plain pasta are recommended.
* **Contraindications:** Laser whitening is not performed on pregnant or nursing mothers (routine clinical safety precaution). Minimum age for cosmetic whitening is 16 years with parental consent.

# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQS)
Q: Does the laser whitening procedure cause sharp tooth sensitivity?
A: No! We isolate your lips and coat your gums with a specialized protective curing barrier before applying the gentle whitening gel. Our cool LED laser accelerates brightening without heating the nerve. Over 95% of our patients experience zero discomfort.

Q: What can I eat after my whitening appointment?
A: We recommend following "The White Diet" for 48 hours. Avoid dark liquids like black coffee, dark tea, red wine, and heavily spiced or colored stews (curry, egusi, tomato stew). White rice, grilled chicken, egg whites, milk, and water are completely safe.

Q: Can I pay for clear aligners in installments?
A: Yes! We offer structured 3-month and 6-month payment plans. You make a 40% initial commitment deposit upon 3D scan approval, and spread the balance comfortably over monthly milestones.

Q: Do you accept walk-ins?
A: We accept same-day emergency walk-ins for severe pain or dental trauma. However, for cosmetic treatments, we strongly encourage booking via WhatsApp to guarantee your chair and prevent wait times.

Q: Can pregnant or breastfeeding mothers do laser teeth whitening?
A: As a medical safety precaution, we advise expectant and nursing mothers to defer elective laser teeth whitening until after lactation. Routine hygiene cleaning (scaling and polishing) remains 100% safe and encouraged during pregnancy.
