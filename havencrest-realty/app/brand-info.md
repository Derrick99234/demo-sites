### 3. Legal Title Verification & Transaction Standards (The Real Lagos Real Estate Details)
* **100% Clean Title Guarantee:**
  * We never list properties with unratified communal claims or disputed ownership.
  * Every property in our portfolio has a certified **Governor’s Consent**, **Certificate of Occupancy (C of O)**, or **Gazetted Excision**.
  * Formal search reports conducted at the Lagos State Lands Bureau (Alausa) are provided to the buyer's lawyer prior to any deposit payment.
* **Transaction & Ancillary Closing Costs (Standard Lagos Fees):**
  * Legal Documentation Fee: 5% of purchase price (Deed of Assignment preparation & sealing).
  * Agency Consultation Fee: 5% of purchase price.
  * Infrastructure / Estate Development Levy: Clearly disclosed upfront with zero hidden charges.
* **Off-Plan Milestone Payment Schedule:**
  * **30% Initial Commitment Deposit:** Upon contract signing and allocation.

# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQS)
Q: How do you verify the legal title before listing a property?
A: Our in-house legal team, led by Folake Adeleke (Esq.), conducts independent searches at the Lagos State Lands Bureau (Alausa). We verify the root of title, check for government acquisitions or court disputes, and provide certified true copies of Governor’s Consent or C of O to your legal counsel.

Q: Can I inspect properties remotely if I live abroad?
A: Yes! Over 60% of our buyers reside overseas. We arrange personalized 4K live video inspections via WhatsApp or Zoom, guided by our engineer who tests water pressure, electrical panels, and finishes room by room.

Q: What are the standard closing fees?
A: In addition to the property cost, standard professional closing fees in Lagos are 5% Legal Documentation Fee (Deed of Assignment preparation & registration) and 5% Agency Fee. All development levies are disclosed upfront in writing.

Q: How do off-plan milestone payment plans work?
A: You start with a 30% initial commitment deposit to lock in your unit price. The remaining 70% is spread across milestone construction stages (carcass, finishing, and key handover) over 6 to 12 months.
