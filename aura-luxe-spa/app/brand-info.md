# FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS (FAQS)
Q: Do I need to bring my own hair extensions for knotless braids?
A: You can bring your own preferred brand (we recommend 3 packs of pre-stretched X-pression 48" or 52"), or you can purchase pre-stretched salon-grade hair directly in our studio for ₦2,500/pack.

Q: Will lash extensions damage my natural lashes?
A: Never. We perform careful single-lash isolation and use feather-light extensions that match the strength of your natural lashes. They shed naturally along with your real lash cycle without pulling.

Q: What happens if I get stuck in Lagos traffic?

Q: How do I book an appointment?
A: Simply message our 24/7 digital assistant on WhatsApp. She will check specialist availability in real time and send you a secure Paystack deposit link to lock in your chair immediately.
