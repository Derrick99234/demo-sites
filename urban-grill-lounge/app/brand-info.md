### 3. Food, Drink & Celebration Policies (The Real Restaurant Details)
* **Dietary & Halal Standards:**
  * All beef, poultry, and lamb cuts are 100% Halal certified.
  * Shellfish and peanut allergy warnings are clearly designated across menu items. Gluten-free pasta is available upon request.
* **Outside Cakes & Birthday Sparklers:**
  * Guests are welcome to bring their own birthday cakes.
  * We provide our **Celebration Cake Service for ₦5,000**, which includes birthday sparklers, presentation on dry ice, festive song from our staff, cutting, and premium dessert plates.
* **Corkage Policy:**
  * Guests wishing to bring bespoke vintage wine or champagne not on our cellar list may do so for a corkage fee of **₦15,000 per 750ml bottle**.
* **Dress Code:**
  * Smart Casual. We request guests refrain from beach shorts, singlets, athletic gym wear, or bathroom slippers in the main dining room and lounge after 6:00 PM.
* **Age & Family Policy:**
  * Families with children are warmly welcomed in the main dining room and outdoor terrace for lunch, dinner, and Sunday brunch (baby high chairs available).
  * On Friday and Saturday nights after 9:00 PM, the VIP Lounge and bar area is strictly 21+.

### 4. Reservation, Deposit & Grace Period Policy
* **Standard Dining Tables (1–4 Guests):** Free instant reservations on WhatsApp. Tables are held for up to 15 minutes past booking time.
* **Group Tables (5+ Guests):** A **₦20,000 table holding deposit** via Paystack is required to guarantee your table during peak evenings (Friday to Sunday).
* **VIP Lounge Booths (Up to 8 Guests):** Require a **₦50,000 booking deposit** with a minimum spend of ₦150,000 toward bottles, platters, and food.
* **100% Bill Credit:** All deposits are 100% credited toward your food and beverage tab upon arrival.
* **Traffic & Late Notice:** If delayed by Lagos traffic, simply text Kayla on WhatsApp and we will hold your table for up to 30 minutes.

### 5. Culinary Leadership
